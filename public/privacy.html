Studora

Your study space, your data.

Studora uses your email address and password to create and secure your account. Authentication is managed by Netlify Identity. Studora's study database does not store your password.

What saves

Your classes, timetable, assignments, notes, goals, reminders, focus history, and appearance settings save to your account in Netlify Database. Attached photos and profile or background images save in private Netlify Blobs storage. Signing in lets you load your saved study space on another device.

Photo recognition

Handwriting + printed-text mode sends your selected photo through Netlify AI Gateway to OpenAI for text recognition. Only use images you are comfortable sending for this purpose. The app does not save scan inputs separately; recognition providers handle them under their applicable data policies. Printed-text mode uses Tesseract on your device and downloads recognition software and language files. You can review and edit the recognized text before saving.

Device storage

The authentication library stores session information on your device to keep you signed in. Older versions of Studora stored study data in browser storage; you can import that data into your account. Importing does not erase the old copy. New study changes save to your account.

Export and deletion

You can export your study space from Backup. To delete your active account and uploaded files, sign in and use Backup → Delete my account. Copies you exported and historical service backups are separate from active account data.
