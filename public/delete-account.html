Delete your Studora account

  1. Open Studora and sign in with your email and password. Use Forgot password if needed.
  2. Open Backup. Export a copy of your data if you want to keep it.
  3. Select Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and select Permanently delete account.

This deletes your active account, study data, and uploaded photos. Deletion cannot be undone. If an error appears, retry while still signed in; some study data may already have been removed.

Previously exported copies and historical platform backups are separate from active account data. Old device-only data from earlier versions remains in the browser until you remove it.
